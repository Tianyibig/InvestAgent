# Diligence question bank (generic)

Adapt to the sector. Prefer measurable answers with sources.

## Technology and product

1. What is self-developed vs licensed? Where is single-supplier risk?
2. What TRL / maturity does each product line sit at?
3. What is MTBF / reliability evidence from real deployments?
4. Patent quality and overseas coverage?
5. Release cadence over the last 12 months?

## Market and customers

1. Top-5 customer revenue concentration?
2. Customer mix (gov / enterprise / consumer / overseas)?
3. Repeat purchase / NRR / backlog growth?
4. DSO trend over three years?
5. Win rate vs named competitors?

## Business model and finance

1. Hardware vs software vs services mix and margin by line?
2. R&D as % of revenue trend?
3. Burn rate and runway?
4. Is unit economics already LTV/CAC > 3x with payback < 18 months?
5. Subsidy dependence of reported profits?

## Team and organization

1. Key-person risk and 12-month attrition of core engineers?
2. Equity incentive and IP assignment clarity?
3. CFO / finance function maturity?

## Compliance and risk

1. Required licenses / export-control exposure?
2. Material litigation or IP disputes?
3. Data / national-security constraints?
4. ESG or supply-chain forced-labor exposure if relevant to exit path?
